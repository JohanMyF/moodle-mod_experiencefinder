# mod_experiencefinder

## ExperienceFinder for Moodle

### Discovering Your Unique SHAPE Through Reflection and Experience

ExperienceFinder is the capstone activity in the SHAPE Discovery Pathway.

The activity helps participants reflect on their life experiences and integrate insights gathered from:

- SelfProfile
- PassionFinder
- SkillsFinder
- PersonalityFinder

The result is a personal SHAPE Discovery Portfolio that supports self-understanding, mentoring conversations, ministry placement discussions, and future service opportunities.

Rather than measuring personality, competency or giftedness, ExperienceFinder helps participants interpret their own story.

---

## Philosophy

ExperienceFinder is based on a simple principle:

> Your future contribution is often hidden within the intersection of what you have consistently done well, what you have consistently enjoyed doing, and what life has taught you.

The activity is designed to help participants discover recurring themes within their experiences and consider how those themes might influence future opportunities for service and growth.

ExperienceFinder does not attempt to assign people to fixed categories.

Instead, it encourages thoughtful reflection, conversation, and experimental placement.

---

## Purpose

ExperienceFinder helps participants:

- Reflect on significant life experiences.
- Identify recurring patterns of success and enjoyment.
- Recognise lessons learned from both positive and difficult experiences.
- Discover evidence of strengths and giftedness.
- Connect previous experiences to future opportunities for service.
- Integrate insights from the other SHAPE instruments.
- Create a personal SHAPE Discovery Portfolio.

---

## Reflection Framework

ExperienceFinder allows teachers to create reflection frameworks consisting of any number of sections and questions.

### Reflection Prompt Lists

Teachers may define lists of experiences intended to stimulate reflection.

Examples include:

- Life experiences
- Family experiences
- Educational experiences
- Spiritual experiences
- Career experiences

These prompts act as memory triggers and may optionally be configured so that participant selections are not stored.

---

### Experience Inventories

Teachers may define lists of experiences that participants can select.

Examples include:

- Previous ministry roles
- Volunteer experiences
- Leadership experiences
- Work experiences
- Service opportunities

Selections may be stored and included in reports.

---

### Reflection Questions

Teachers may create any number of reflection questions.

Examples include:

- What experiences have shaped your life most significantly?
- What have you consistently enjoyed doing?
- What strengths appear repeatedly in your story?
- What kinds of people do you enjoy helping?

Responses may be edited and revisited over time.

---

## Integration With Other SHAPE Instruments

ExperienceFinder can display summary information from:

### SelfProfile

Personal strengths and characteristics.

### PassionFinder

Interests, motivations and sources of energy.

### SkillsFinder

Competencies, abilities and practical skills.

### PersonalityFinder

Personality preferences and ministry style.

---

## SHAPE Discovery Portfolio

The final output of ExperienceFinder is a personal SHAPE Discovery Portfolio.

The portfolio combines:

- Life experiences
- Service experiences
- Reflection responses
- SelfProfile results
- PassionFinder results
- SkillsFinder results
- PersonalityFinder results

---

## Visual Integration

ExperienceFinder may incorporate visual summaries from previous instruments, including:

- SelfProfile strength charts
- PassionFinder passion visualisations
- SkillsFinder competency maps
- PersonalityFinder personality matrix

These visual elements help participants view their SHAPE as an integrated whole.

---

## Participant Reflection

For each area of the portfolio, teachers may define reflection questions that invite participants to:

- Confirm results
- Challenge results
- Add context
- Record observations
- Identify recurring themes

The emphasis is on interpretation rather than assessment.

---

## Personal Mission and Next Steps

ExperienceFinder concludes by encouraging participants to reflect on questions such as:

- What themes appear repeatedly in my life?
- What gives me energy?
- What opportunities should I explore next?
- How might God be calling me to serve?

The objective is to move participants from self-discovery toward active contribution.

---

## Sharing Instruments

ExperienceFinder is not JSON-driven. Teachers share configured activities using standard Moodle backup, restore, duplicate and import processes.

This keeps the plugin focused on a Moodle-native conversation framework rather than a portable assessment schema.

---

## Educational Applications

ExperienceFinder can be used in:

- Churches
- Ministry training programmes
- Volunteer development
- Career guidance programmes
- Mentoring relationships
- Leadership development initiatives
- Educational institutions
- Community organisations

---

## Key Principle

ExperienceFinder helps people recognise that their experiences are not random events but valuable clues.

By reflecting on where they have consistently succeeded, what they have consistently enjoyed, and how they have grown through life's challenges, participants gain a clearer understanding of how they may best contribute to the people and communities around them.
